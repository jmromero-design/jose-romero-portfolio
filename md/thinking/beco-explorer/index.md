# Explore the _BECO graph._

## How to _read it._

BECO is a knowledge graph I'm building to check which behavioural-science claims hold up before they end up in a design decision. The map draws all of it at once: the eight clusters at the centre, then domains, topics and named concepts, then the findings and real-world examples behind each concept, and at the edge the sources and the people who wrote them. The cards beside it read whatever you pick.

Nothing is placed by hand. Each item sits near what it's linked to, so concepts that share topics, studies or authors end up close together, and the teal arcs connect concepts the research treats as related. It's a snapshot I regenerate after each research round, not a live database.

- Solid teal: the research supports it.

- Pale teal: partly supported, with caveats worth knowing.

- Dashed outline: no rigorous backing found yet.

- Red ring: often repeated in design circles in a form the studies don't support.

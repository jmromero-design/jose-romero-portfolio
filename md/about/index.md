### AI practice

#### AI facilitation

Turning what AI produces into material a team can read, discuss and act on. Most of my facilitation now is structuring and decoding AI output so a team can take it in without overload and act on it.

#### AI workflow design

Multi-agent workflows with hard human checkpoints, built on Shape Up. The full framework is in [Designing with AI](https://www.joseromerodesign.com/thinking/ai-workflow-framework/).

#### Context and skill building

Writing the skills, project instructions and memory that make agents work the team’s way: tone, standards, and what to check before handing anything back.

#### Agentic reporting

Reports, decks and dashboards built by agents in VS Code, connected to Jira, Confluence and Microsoft 365. It has almost replaced my office tools.

#### Designing in code

Prototypes and production UI built with AI, with Figma connected to the code. This site is one of them.

#### Knowledge systems

Research tooling that grades its own evidence. [BECO](https://www.joseromerodesign.com/thinking/beco-explorer/) links 420 behavioural-science sources to 243 concepts in a Neo4j graph.

### Design and research

#### Product design

End-to-end product design across web and mobile, from research and flows to prototypes and specs engineering can build from.

#### Experimentation and CRO

Hypotheses, A/B and multivariate tests, and results that hold up in front of stakeholders. I still run experiments in Optimizely and ContentSquare on the Second-hand Marketplace.

#### Behavioural economics

Defaults, friction, loss aversion and social proof applied to real design decisions, with BECO as the evidence behind them.

#### UX research

Moderated and unmoderated research, in-store sessions, heuristic reviews and WCAG audits, turned into direction a team can use.

#### Design operations

Ways of working across Figma, Jira, Confluence and Phrase, and the stakeholder work that keeps product, engineering and business moving, in English and Spanish.

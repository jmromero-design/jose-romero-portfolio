Privacy

What this site does
with your data.

Last updated: August 2026

This is a personal portfolio site, not a product with an account system, so there isn't much to collect. Here's exactly what happens.

Analytics

This site uses Vercel Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to understand which pages get read and how fast they load. Both are privacy-preserving by design: they don't use cookies, don't track you across other sites, and report aggregated, anonymised numbers, not individual visitor profiles. I can't see your name, your IP address, or anything that identifies you personally from this data.

Cookies and local storage

This site doesn't set any cookies. Your browser's local storage is used for exactly two things: remembering whether you prefer dark or light mode, and which language you last viewed the site in. Both stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Forms and contact

There are no forms on this site. Every "Contact" or "Send an email" link opens your own email client via a standard mailto: link, so nothing you write is submitted to or stored by this site. If you email me, that message lives in my inbox like any other email, governed by whatever email provider you use.

Third parties

This site is hosted on Vercel, which serves the pages and runs the analytics above. Links to LinkedIn take you to LinkedIn's own site, governed by LinkedIn's own privacy policy, not this one.

Your rights

Since this site doesn't collect or store personal data of its own, there's nothing on file to access, correct, or delete. If you have any question about this notice, or about an email you've sent me, reach out at joseromero.next@gmail.com.

    How to
    read it.

    BECO is a knowledge graph I'm building to check which behavioural-science claims hold up before they end up in a design decision. This map draws all of it at once. The eight clusters sit at the centre, with domains and topics around them, then the named concepts, then the findings and real-world examples behind each concept, and at the edge the sources and the people who wrote them.

    Nothing is placed by hand. Each item sits near what it's linked to, so concepts that share topics, studies or authors end up close together, and the teal arcs connect concepts the research treats as related.

    • Solid teal: the research supports it.
    • Pale teal: partly supported, with caveats worth knowing.
    • Dashed outline: no rigorous backing found yet.
    • Red ring: often repeated in design circles in a form the studies don't support.
